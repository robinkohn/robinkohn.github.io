About Us

About T & R Formwork

T & R Formwork is a specialist formwork company providing a range of services to the building and construction industry. Based in Perth, Western Australia, we work as a concrete formwork contractor, frequently collaborating with high-end residential builders.

Established in 2010 and led by Toby Sturk and Roger Stenmark, our experienced team works together to deliver quality workmanship and reliable service on every project.

Formwork & Concrete Services in Perth

From suspended slabs to architectural finishes, we build the moulds, support frameworks and reinforcing that Perth builders need to pour with confidence.

Suspended Slab Formwork

Constructing the temporary moulds and support frameworks used to form elevated concrete floors, engineered to spec and stripped on schedule.

Architectural Concrete

Building detailed off-form concrete elements, radius and curved walls, and specialised upstands, finished to an exposed architectural standard.

Custom Stairs

Forming complex concrete staircases with intricate detailing — from straight flights to curved and cantilevered designs.

Residential Builds

Collaborating with local custom home builders and engineering groups across the Perth metropolitan area.
